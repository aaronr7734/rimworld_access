RimWorld Access - Changelog for Aaron's Unofficial Patch
06/22/2026

CHANGES
-------
- Terrain sounds no longer play for tiles you have not discovered yet. Moving
  the cursor onto a fogged tile is now silent, so the terrain audio can no
  longer hint at what is hidden under the fog of war.
- Fixed the background music not starting after you create a new world and
  colony. Previously you had to restart the game before the music would play
  again. The guitar sting that plays when you close the scenario introduction
  dialog now plays again as well. The game still starts paused after the intro
  so you have time to get your bearings before time begins.
- Fixed entering custom numbers for bills at a workstation. Typing a quantity,
  such as a target count or how many times to do a bill, now goes into the
  number field instead of being swallowed by the menu's type-to-search.
- Fixed the research detail view mixing up which buildings were available. A
  research bench you have actually built now reads as available even when a
  required facility like the multi-analyzer is missing, and each required
  facility now reports its own status: available, present but inactive, or not
  available.
- Simplified the map cursor jump modes down to the two useful ones, a preset
  distance and a jump to the next tile beside a wall. Hold Shift and press Up
  or Down to switch between them, and Shift with Left or Right to change the
  preset distance.
- The world map tile information (press 4 on the world map) no longer reads out
  the internal Tile ID, which was developer data not meant for players.
- Added a Roofs category to the map scanner. It collects natural roofs grouped
  into patches like terrain, with an All view plus two groups: overhead
  mountain (thick) roofs and thin rock roofs. Use it to find where you are
  standing under a mountain.
- The trade window and the info card now play their normal close sound when you
  close them, matching what a sighted player hears, instead of closing silently.
- Fixed a doubled announcement when opening a menu from a building's inspection.
  Opening Bills, Storage, Temperature and similar categories announced both the
  opened menu and the inspection row behind it (for example "Add bill... 1 of 6.
  Bills. 2 of 3"). The inspection row no longer speaks over the menu you opened.
- The mod info panel now shows the project website (rimworldaccess.com) as a
  link, and the mod description was rewritten to be shorter and clearer.

06/21/2026

CHANGES
-------
- The mod is now available in Latin American Spanish (Espanol Latinoamerica).
  Every menu, announcement, and tooltip is translated, along with the Learning
  Helper documentation chapters and the written hair, beard, and tattoo style
  descriptions. Choose it from the language list and RimWorld Access speaks
  Spanish throughout. As with our other community translations, this is a machine
  translation anchored to RimWorld's own official Spanish wording, so corrections
  from native speakers are very welcome.
- Styling stations are now fully accessible. When a colonist uses "Change
  style" at a styling station, a keyboard menu opens. Press Tab to move between
  the hair, beard, face tattoo, body tattoo, and apparel color sections, and use
  the arrow keys (and type to search) to browse the options. Pressing Enter on a
  style applies it and then opens a color list so you can set its color in one
  step. The currently applied style announces its color, so you always know what
  you have on. Press Alt+S to apply all your changes, right bracket to reset, and
  Escape to cancel.
- Pawns now have an Appearance section in their inspection (press Enter on a
  colonist, then find Appearance). It lists their hair and hair color, beard,
  tattoos, and favorite color in one place, so you can review how someone looks
  without opening the styling station.
- Every hairstyle, beard, and tattoo now has a written description of what it
  actually looks like, so you are no longer choosing by name alone. For example,
  the princess hairstyle reads as long hair parted down the center that falls
  past the shoulders, and the skeleton body tattoo as a skull above a curved
  ribcage. These descriptions are spoken after the style name in the styling
  station, in a pawn's Appearance section, and in the ideoligion viewer and
  editor where you set how often each style appears, so you always know what you
  are picking. The name comes first and the description follows, so you can move
  on as soon as you have heard enough.
- You can now choose paint colors when painting floors or buildings. Selecting
  the Paint Floor or Paint Building tool opens a list of every paint color by
  name - red, scarlet, plum, and so on - so you can pick one and paint as usual.
  Press C while painting to change the color again. As you move the map cursor,
  painted floors and buildings now announce their color too (for example, "fine
  marble tile, deep purple").
- Plans are now fully accessible. When you pick the Plan tool from the architect
  Orders menu, a color list opens so you can choose the plan color by name
  (gray, red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue, purple, or pink); press C while
  drawing to change it again. As you move the map cursor, any plan under it
  announces its color and name, for example "purple Potato farm". Plans have
  their own scanner category: each block of plan is one entry that reads its
  color, name, and size, and Home jumps you to the nearest edge and then the
  center, just like terrain patches. The Plans category also has a subcategory
  for each color in use, so you can browse just your red plans, just your blue
  ones, and so on. Press G on a plan to manage it, the same as any other object:
  the gizmo list lets you rename it, change its color, show or hide it, expand or
  shrink it, copy it, or delete it. Copy captures the plan, then Ctrl+V pastes a
  duplicate. Paste is cursor-relative: the cell you were standing on when you
  copied lands right under your cursor when you paste, so the duplicate appears
  exactly where you expect. If the paste covers cells that belonged to another
  plan, it tells you how many it replaced. Pressing Enter on a plan still reads
  its overview (color, name, and size).
- The in-game Learning Helper now speaks your language in Chinese, Ukrainian,
  and Russian. This covers both the mod's own accessibility lessons (inspecting
  things, the scanner, selecting colonists, building, and so on) and the
  rewritten help for the base-game and DLC concepts, so the lessons you read
  with the ? key are no longer in English on those languages.
- A few newer lines now translate as well: the shot report you hear when aiming
  a drafted colonist, the scanner's polluted-area results, and the world-map
  "jumped N tiles" announcement.

06/12/2026

ANNOUNCEMENTS
-------------
- Localization support begins today, as an early beta. The mod has been
  massively refactored so that everything it speaks can be translated, and
  two trial languages are included to start: Simplified Chinese and
  Ukrainian. A lot of strings are going to be missing translations at first
  (the mod speaks English wherever one is missing), so please report the
  ones you catch. I will not be expanding to more languages until players
  have caught most of them, because every missing string found later has to
  be fixed across every supported language at once. Once the trial languages
  stabilize, localization across all 29 of the game's supported languages is
  coming. To set expectations: the base game is not localized perfectly in
  all 29 languages, the DLCs are not localized in all 29 either, and neither
  are most third-party mods. I cannot help with any of that; the game's own
  localization is a community project, and the README document in the game's
  installation folder lists where to go if you want to contribute.
- I intend to release this update officially by Sunday, documentation
  included. Steam Workshop support may not make it by Sunday, but the
  release itself will be a complete, official update with everything here
  built in.

CHANGES
-------
- Improved the process of planting Gauranlen trees and other plantable
  seeds. While planting, a temporary scanner category now lists every tile
  you can plant on, with a second subcategory holding only the spots far
  enough from buildings to avoid the reduced connection strength warning.
  Arrow keys call out each tile's plantability as you sweep, and cancelling
  that warning dialog now returns you to placement mode instead of throwing
  away the whole planting order.
- Anomaly entity suppression: you can now toggle auto-suppression on or off for
  a held entity. On its activity gizmo, Enter toggles auto-suppression and right
  bracket still adjusts the suppression threshold, matching the psyfocus gizmo's

- Potentially fixed situations where a pawn could still be reported as
  downed after reloading a save in which they are no longer downed. I was

05/31/2026

CHANGES
-------
- The Archonexus relocation endgame is now fully keyboard-accessible:
  choosing your colonists and items, picking your
  new colony's world tile, and reforming your ideoligion all work with the mod.
- During that endgame you can create, load, or fully edit your new colony's
  ideoligion, just like the builder you use when starting a world.

- Arrow-key navigation now names the roof above you instead of a generic
  "roofed" or "underground", so a thin rock roof, a solid overhead mountain,
  and a player-built roof are easy to tell apart.
- Fish population reports moved from the terrain key (2) to the resource key
  (3), alongside plants and minerals. Key 3 now lists only the resources
  relevant to your tile.
- You can now banish a colonist, and execute a guilty one.
  Both live on the Social section of a pawn's inspection screen and on the
  Actions tab of their info card, each with the game's own warning about what
  will happen.
- The scanner now points you at the nearest tile of a patch (a biome, a vein
  of ore, a stretch of soil, a road or river) instead of its center, so you
  hear how far its closest edge is and whether you are already standing on it.
  Press Home to jump to that nearest tile; once you are on the patch, press
  Home again to jump to its center. The colony map and the world map work the
  same way.

- You can now load colonists and items into a map portal the same way you load
  a caravan or transport pod. This covers entering an ancient complex, going
  down an Anomaly pit gate or insect lair, and hauling your loot back out
  through the exit. The enter and exit commands now open a Pawns and Items
  screen with full keyboard navigation. Before, that screen opened silently and
  you could only move pawns in one at a time through the right-bracket menu,
  with no way to bring items along.

BUG FIXES
---------
- In auto-jump mode, the scanner no longer reports every place it takes you as
  being right on top of you. It used to measure the distance after it had
  already moved you onto the target, so each jump sounded like it was zero
  tiles away. It now simply announces what is on each tile as you flip through
  the scanner.
- Cleaned up the building inspection menu: the "Power Control", "Breakdown
  Status", and "Power" items are gone. They either duplicated the Overview
  or let you do things you shouldn't (flip power instantly). Use the toggle
  power command (G) and a colonist will flick the switch as normal.
- Number keys, arrow keys, and letter shortcuts no longer "leak" through to
  whatever is underneath an open menu. With a menu open, pressing a number
  could announce tile info, and letter shortcuts (like Alt+A or the ] orders key) could fire or
  type into a search box. This is now theoretically fixed across every menu.
- Typeahead search now works the same in every menu. While you are searching,
  Up/Down step through the matches and Home/End jump to the first and last,
  all without clearing your search, and each tells you where you are (for
  example "match 2 of 3").
- Typing no longer jumps to the wrong list when one menu sits on top of
  another. Float menus opened over a menu (like the bills "add recipe"
  picker), menus opened over a pawn's inspection screen (Operations, surgery
  pickers, Health Settings), and the quantity boxes in caravan and pod-loading
  dialogs now all search the menu you are actually looking at.
- The pawn and building inspection screens now support typeahead. Start typing
  to jump through the tree, including weapons, apparel, and inventory items by
  name. Inspection search also matches what's inside a node, not just its name.
- In the drug policy editor, Escape now clears an active search before closing,
  and the drug list reports how many matches your search found. The
  growing-zone plant picker also steps through search results correctly.
- The saved-ideoligion load list no longer locks up when it's empty; Escape
  closes it normally.
- When editing an ideoligion's base precepts (cannibalism, corpses, drug use,
  and the like), you can now press Delete to remove a precept you set,
  following the game's own rules about which can be dropped.
- Fixed Alt-key shortcuts like Alt+R not working on some non-US keyboard
  layouts, including AZERTY.

05/25/2026

This is a milestone beta release for me. As of this update, every RimWorld DLC is
essentially fully accessible with the mod. Anything still rough is
either a convenience (mostly around gravship building) or a bug, and
bugs get fixed as they come in. The headline this time is the custom
ideoligion builder, but it was only possible because of a big overhaul
to how text boxes work, so let's start there.

CHANGES
-------
- Text boxes are now real text boxes, or as close as we can get. There
  is an actual cursor you can move through your text with the arrow
  keys, or jump word by word by holding Ctrl. Hold Shift while moving to
  select text, and Backspace removes the character before the cursor (or
  whatever you have selected). The mod now also knows the difference
  between a single-line box, like a name, and a multi-line box, like a
  description, so in a multi-line box you can press Shift+Enter to start
  a new line. When you finish editing with Enter, the mod reads back what
  the field now says. Typeahead should also work on
  non-English keyboard layouts now, though I have not been able to test
  that myself.

- The big one: you can now build your own custom ideoligions, just like
  sighted players do on the ideoligion setup screen. This has been a
  long time coming. The builder has two tabs, and you switch between them
  with Tab. The first tab lists every ideoligion in the game; the second
  tab shows whichever one is selected. Select your own ideoligion and the
  second tab becomes a full editor; select someone else's and it is a
  read-only viewer. The editor is one big expandable list. The game crams
  an enormous amount of information onto this screen, so when you expand
  an item you can arrow through it line by line and hear all of it at your
  own pace. Most items can be changed by pressing Enter on them, including
  your ideoligion's name and descriptions, so when in doubt, press Enter
  and see what happens. To move on to the next setup screen, press Alt+S.
  The right bracket key ( ] ) opens a menu of extra options, including
  saving an ideoligion you have built so you can load it again in future
  games.
- Fair warning: building an ideoligion is genuinely complicated, even for
  sighted players. There are a LOT of moving parts. If you are new to the
  game, you may want to get comfortable first. But I'm not your mom. Do
  whatever you want!
- Fluid ideoligions work the same way in-game. Once yours has earned
  enough development points to reform, open the ideoligion viewer and
  press Enter on the item that shows your development points to begin.
- The focused Work view now reads the selected colonist's name when it
  opens.
- The colonist skills table (Alt+P) now tells you how much experience
  each skill has toward its next level, not just the level. On a paused
  game you can rest on a skill and hear the numbers change as colonists
  learn.
- The starting characters screen now gives each colonist a Relations
  category, listing who they're tied to in your starting group and how
  - "Daughter," "Lover," and the like - with each pawn's opinion in the
  tooltip. Press Enter on a relation to jump straight to that pawn.
- That same screen now has a Team Skills tab. Press Tab to switch to it
  for a quick rundown of which starting pawn is best at each skill.
- The world map scanner now finds rivers, not just roads.
- Walls (built walls, natural rock, and closed doors) now play their own
  navigation sound instead of the sound of the ground beneath them.
  Terrain sounds were also evened out so the volumes are more consistent.

BUG FIXES
---------
- Returning to the main menu from inside a game while on the world map no longer leaves the menu
  unresponsive. The Enter key works again right away, so you can pick a
  menu option without getting stuck.

BUG FIXES
---------
- Screen reader speech works again on setups where JAWS (or another

04/24/2026

The headline change: you can finally name your saves. The pause
menu and the save/load screens also got a thorough polish pass.


NEW FEATURES
------------
- You can now type a custom save name. The save menu drops you
  in a text field pre-filled with your faction name -- type to
  replace it, or press Down arrow to pick an existing save to
  overwrite.

- Save and load menus now announce each save's game version and
  flag autosaves, matching what sighted players see. Loading a
  save from a different version (or with a different mod list)
  now goes through RimWorld's compatibility warning dialog, and
  pressing Escape on that dialog returns you to the save list
  instead of dropping you out to the main menu.

- Anomaly research projects announce their tier (basic or
  advanced) while you browse the research menu, so you know
  which kind of entity you'll need to study to advance them.


PAUSE MENU POLISH
-----------------
- Cursor position is preserved when you Escape out of a sub-menu
  (Save, Load, Options, Play Settings, Review Scenario), so you
  land back on the item you just used. Closing the pause menu
  with Escape resets the cursor for next time.

- Quit to Main Menu and Quit to Desktop now use proper
  confirmation dialogs (matching the rest of the mod), and
  cancelling either one returns you to the pause menu where you
  left off.

- Play Settings now returns you to the pause menu on Escape
  instead of kicking you out entirely.

- Home and End jump to the first and last items in the pause
  menu.


More BUG FIXES
---------
- Starting an Anomaly research project no longer falsely claims
  your regular research project was paused -- Anomaly and
  regular research run on separate tracks, and the mod now only
  mentions a previous project when one was genuinely replaced.

- Several save/load labels that were hardcoded English
  (Overwrite, Load, Save, "please enter a name", delete
  confirmation) now use RimWorld's translation keys, so
  non-English players hear them in their own language.

A NOTE ON FOOTSTEPS
-------------------
The positional footstep system from the 04/10 patch has been removed in
this release.

After living with it for a few days, I couldn't get footsteps to the
quality bar I wanted to put my name on. RimWorld's sound stage fights
the integration: footstep positions don't track with a pawn's other
sounds (so a cooking pawn who starts walking sounds out of place relative to its cooking sound), and reverb behavior was inconsistent in ways I couldn't tame.
I had hoped the feature would be most useful in combat, but even after
a lot of time tuning per-category volume sliders, footsteps either
drowned out game audio, shattering immersion, or were too
quiet to carry information. There was no middle ground I was happy to
ship.

Huge thanks to OlegTheSnowman, who did the original contribution this
system was built on. Oleg is of course free to continue this work in
their own mod, as they were doing before. I may return to positional
audio later, but for now I'd rather focus on the systems and DLC
coverage already in the mod, documentation, and the long tail of bugs.

MAP AND TILE INFORMATION
------------------------
The way items on a map tile are reported has been completely overhauled.
Previously, items were broken into separate categories (pawns, buildings,
items, plants) with arbitrary caps — for example, only the first 2
buildings and 3-5 pawns would be announced, and plants were hidden

  4. Buildings (structures, furniture, doors)
  5. Plants (trees, crops, wild plants)
  6. Conduits (hidden infrastructure)

end. Cell-targeted orders like mine or smooth floor are announced at

SCHEDULE MENU (F2) — REDESIGNED
-------------------------------
The schedule menu has been completely redesigned. It now uses a brush-
based painting system that lets you assign jobs across hours quickly
using keyboard shortcuts.

How it works:
The schedule is a grid where each row is a pawn and each column is an
hour of the day (0–23). Each cell can be set to a job type like
Anything, Work, Sleep, Recreation, or Meditate (if you have the Royalty DLC).

Selecting a brush:
Press number keys 1 through 4 (or 5 with Royalty) to select a brush.
The brushes correspond to the available job types:
  1 = Anything
  2 = Work (if available)
  3 = Sleep
  4 = Recreation
  5 = Meditate (Royalty DLC)
The selected brush is announced when you pick it.

Applying the brush:
- Space or Enter: Apply the brush to the current cell
- Shift+Left/Right: Move to the next/previous hour AND apply the brush
  as you go — this is "painting." Hold Shift and arrow across hours to
  quickly set a range.
- Shift+Home/End: Paint from the current cell to the first/last hour
  of the row
- Ctrl+Shift+Home/End: Paint every row at the selected column with the active brush.

For example: Let's say you have three pawns, Bob, Alice and John:

It's currently 11:43 in the game, and they're all having a really difficulty day, so you want to allow them to have recreation time the next two hours. You'd press f2, hit the right arrow key once (to navigate to hour 12 then press 3 to select the recreation brush.

From here, you could do a few different things:

1. You could press space on hour 12 to paint that single tile with your recreation brush.
2. You could tap shift right arrow, to select both hours 12 and 1 with your recreation brush (for your first pawn.)
3. Or, you could press control shift end. This would paint hour 12 with your recreation brush for all three of your pawns. Then you'd just have to hit the right arrow key to select hour 13 and use control shift home to paint all three pawns with recreation for hour 13.

If you'd rather just use] space to set individual cells, that's perfectly fine too. You can also still use control c and control v to copy and paste schedules between pawns. 

If this proves to be confusing for folks (I'm assuming it will) I can make a video about it if Liam doesn't! I also will be updating the README to describe this screen in more detail.

Feedback is always welcome!

Navigation:
- Up/Down arrows move between pawns
- Left/Right arrows move between hours
- Tab/Shift+Tab switches between Schedule and Allowed Areas columns
- Home/End jump to the first/last hour
- Typeahead search to find pawns by name

INFO CARDS EVERYWHERE (Alt+I)
-----------------------------
The biggest addition in this patch for Vanilla Rimworld: you can now press Alt+I in nearly any
menu or screen to open the info card for whatever you have selected. Info
cards are the detailed stat sheets that sighted players access by clicking
the (i) button -- and until now, screen reader users had few ways to reach
them. They're now available in 15 different contexts:

- Map cursor (when hovering over buildings, items, terrain, or pawns)
- Architect menu (get stats on a building before you place it)
- Plant selection menu
- Storage settings (check details on any item in the filter list)
- Bills menu and bill configuration
- Gizmo bar (abilities, build commands, and more)
- Colony inventory
- Research menu and research detail view (inspect unlocked items and recipes)
- Colony animals menu
- Wildlife menu
- Float menus (right-click context menus)
- Pawn inspection: gear, health conditions, and genes all support Alt+I
- Inside info cards themselves -- follow hyperlinks to drill into related entries

Some items (especially within info cards) that have an info card available will say "Inspectable" when you
navigate to them, so you always know when Alt+I will work. I want to extend this across the entire mod, but right now, things that are very clearly items / buildings / plants don't say this since it should be assumed that you can press alt i.

Nested info card navigation:
- When an info card mentions another item (like a material or a
  research project), you can press Alt+I again to open that item's info card
- Press Escape to go back to the previous card -- your cursor position is
  remembered
- When a stat entry links to multiple items, a selection menu appears so you
  can choose which one to inspect

Stat entries that can be expanded now correctly detect whether they actually
have explanation text, so you won't see "No items to show" on entries that
have nothing to expand.
