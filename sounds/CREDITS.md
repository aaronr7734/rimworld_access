# Sound credits

These terrain sounds come from [Freesound](https://freesound.org). Each one is released under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), so no attribution is required, but the people who recorded them deserve the thanks anyway. Every file is a single step cut from a longer recording and leveled to match the other terrain sounds.

- `sand.wav`: [Footsteps on Sand](https://freesound.org/people/amholma/sounds/376797/) by amholma
- `rough stone.wav`: [Hiking Boot Footsteps on Stone](https://freesound.org/people/Fission9/sounds/521590/) by Fission9
- `smooth stone.wav`: [Footstep on Stone 3](https://freesound.org/people/sgossner/sounds/197780/) by sgossner, with some of the low end filtered out
- `ice.wav`: [Walking on Crunchy Ice (01/30/2014)](https://freesound.org/people/courter/sounds/447822/) by courter
- `lava.wav`: [Acid Bubbling](https://freesound.org/people/spookymodem/sounds/202094/) by spookymodem
- `marsh.wav`: [Walking on wet and muddy marsh land with clothing rustle](https://freesound.org/people/jackmichaelking/sounds/559820/) by jackmichaelking
- `metal flooring.wav`: [Walking on metal](https://freesound.org/people/Sanderboah/sounds/696374/) by Sanderboah
