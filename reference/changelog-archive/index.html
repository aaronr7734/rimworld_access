    Changelog Archive (Pre-2.0)¶

    The history of RimWorld Access before version 2.0, from the "Aaron's Unofficial Patch" era, preserved verbatim. Newest first.

    06/26/2026¶

    CHANGES¶

    • Fixed digits typed into a quantity chooser also passing through to the screen behind it. Typeahead no longer leaks past quantity choosers.
    • Fixed the inventory showing item locations ("on", "carried by", etc.) in English for non-English players.

    06/24/2026¶

    CHANGES¶

    • Fixed a freeze in the custom ideoligion builder where, after typing a name, adjective, or member name in Simplified Chinese and pressing Enter, the mod stopped responding to keys until you switched windows and back.
    • Fixed body parts in the health tab reading their condition and injuries twice.

    06/22/2026¶

    CHANGES¶

    • Fixed the background music not starting after you create a new world and colony. You no longer have to restart the game for it to play.
    • Fixed entering custom numbers for bills at a workstation. Typing a quantity now goes into the number field instead of being swallowed by the menu's typeahead.
    • Fixed the research detail view mixing up which buildings were available. A bench you have built now reads as available even when a required facility like the multi-analyzer is missing, and each facility reports its own status: available, present but inactive, or not available.
    • Terrain sounds no longer play for tiles you have not discovered yet.
    • The world map tile information (press 4) no longer reads the internal Tile ID, which was developer data not meant for players.
    • Styling stations are now fully accessible. Tab moves between the hair, beard, face tattoo, body tattoo, and apparel color sections, and the arrow keys (with typeahead) browse the options. Enter applies a style and opens a color list so you can set its color in one step. Alt+S applies all your changes; Escape cancels.
    • Plans are now fully accessible. A new Plans scanner category groups your plans by color into subcategories, and after placing a plan you can configure it from the gizmo menu.
    • Every hairstyle, beard, and tattoo now has a written description of what it actually looks like, so you are no longer choosing by name alone.
    • Pawns now have an Appearance section in their inspect screen, listing hair and hair color, beard, tattoos, and favorite color in one place.
    • The paint designator's color picker is now accessible, and painted floors and buildings announce their color as you move the cursor over them.
    • Added a Roofs category to the scanner, covering both thin rock roofs and overhead mountains.
    • The Learning Helper now includes RimWorld Access lessons, and most of the game's own lessons have been edited with the mod in mind. Suggestions for more are welcome.
    • With a drafted pawn selected on the colonist bar, pressing 1 on a tile holding another pawn reads the game's shooting accuracy tooltip.
    • Removed the old unsupported cursor jump modes; only "preset distance" and "adjacent to walls" remain.
    • The mod info panel now shows the project website (rimworldaccess.com) as a link, and the description was rewritten to be shorter.
    • The mod is now available in Latin American Spanish and Russian. Like our other community translations, these are machine translations anchored to RimWorld's own official wording, so corrections from native speakers are welcome.

    06/17/2026¶

    CHANGES¶

    • Added cursor sound for vacstone tiles.
    • On space and asteroid maps, the tile info key that reports light and temperature (4) now also reports the vacuum level for the current tile, shown only when there is vacuum present.
    • Fixed one more English leak reported by a Chinese player: the "X available" count on items in the form-caravan list now speaks in your language.
    • Fixed the G gizmo menu after a Home-key or scanner jump: it now shows the gizmos for the tile you landed on, not a colonist you had selected earlier.
    • Fixed the work priorities menu opening broken when a mech was selected; pressing F1 or Alt+W on a mech now opens on a real colonist instead.
    • Fixed map switching (Shift+Period / Shift+Comma) skipping loaded maps with no colonists, mechs, or animals, such as a gravship map left holding only a grav anchor after everyone shuttled off.

    06/16/2026¶

    CHANGES¶

    • Players using a Chinese, Japanese, or Korean input method can now search lists again. Instant type-ahead can't compose pinyin, so you open a search box on purpose: press / in a list menu (trade, animals, research, and so on) or Z for the map scanner, type your query, then press Enter or Escape to close it. Everyone else keeps the instant type-ahead they already had, unchanged.

    06/15/2026¶

    CHANGES¶

    • Fixed Alt+letter shortcuts (Alt+B/Alt+A in trade, Alt+M in work priorities) breaking for players using a Chinese/Japanese/Korean input method.
    • Translated two more spots that spoke English: the "active/not set" summary in the custom ideoligion precepts list, and the "material" cost word on the architect build menu.
    • Fixed "Refuel from cargo" on passenger shuttles: typing an amount and pressing Enter once now correctly applies it and confirms.

    06/14/2026¶

    CHANGES¶

    • Fixed type-ahead search in the info card. Some idiot (me) forgot to register the info card with the new type-ahead system I built, so typing a letter in the info card either did nothing (when opened with Alt+I) or jumped around the inspection screen underneath it. It is now registered and works correctly.
    • Tracked down more places where the mod still spoke English instead of your language, thanks to reports from players running it in Chinese. Fixed: the expand/collapse state on memes in the custom ideoligion editor; the "Name" column header in the skills table and the assign menu; the generic "material" cost word shown for a wall or other stuff-built thing before you pick a material; the "Faction Name" text-box label when naming your faction; the "Rename" label on the zone and storage-group rename buttons; the sort direction ("ascending"/"descending") read out when you sort a table column; the on/off state on toggle buttons in command menus; and the "Preview/Stop ritual sound" option in the ideoligion editor. Keep the reports coming.
    • The world route planner now reads its path directions in your language too. When you trace a planned route tile by tile, the "head north to waypoint 2" style guidance (and the "Destination" call-out at the end) was still coming out in English. The compass direction and the whole phrasing around it are now translated.

    06/13/2026¶

    CHANGES¶

    • The Character tab now reads a pawn's age and birthday: their biological age (with chronological age alongside it when the two differ), the date they were born, and the full chronological and biological breakdown. It also now reads the rest of what the vanilla character card shows but we were leaving out: backstory title, what the pawn is incapable of, royal titles, ideoligion role, xenotype, and any abilities listed on the card. The pawn's info card ('i') Character tab gained the same age and birthday details, so both places match the game.
    • You can now type Simplified Chinese, Japanese, and Korean into the mod's text boxes and search fields. This is new, so if you use one of those languages, please let me know whether typing and searching work for you.

    06/12/2026¶

    ANNOUNCEMENTS¶

    • Localization support begins today, as an early beta. The mod has been massively refactored so that everything it speaks can be translated, and two trial languages are included to start: Simplified Chinese and Ukrainian. A lot of strings are going to be missing translations at first (the mod speaks English wherever one is missing), so please report the ones you catch. I will not be expanding to more languages until players have caught most of them, because every missing string found later has to be fixed across every supported language at once. Once the trial languages stabilize, localization across all 29 of the game's supported languages is coming. To set expectations: the base game is not localized perfectly in all 29 languages, the DLCs are not localized in all 29 either, and neither are most third-party mods. I cannot help with any of that; the game's own localization is a community project, and the README document in the game's installation folder lists where to go if you want to contribute.
    • I intend to release this update officially by Sunday, documentation included. Steam Workshop support may not make it by Sunday, but the release itself will be a complete, official update with everything here built in.

    CHANGES¶

    • Improved the process of planting Gauranlen trees and other plantable seeds. While planting, a temporary scanner category now lists every tile you can plant on, with a second subcategory holding only the spots far enough from buildings to avoid the reduced connection strength warning. Arrow keys call out each tile's plantability as you sweep, and cancelling that warning dialog now returns you to placement mode instead of throwing away the whole planting order.
    • Anomaly entity suppression: you can now toggle auto-suppression on or off for a held entity. On its activity gizmo, Enter toggles auto-suppression and right bracket still adjusts the suppression threshold, matching the psyfocus gizmo's Enter-and-slider layout. Previously only the threshold was reachable.
    • Potentially fixed situations where a pawn could still be reported as downed after reloading a save in which they are no longer downed. I was never able to reproduce the problem myself, so I cannot promise the fix works; if you have run into it, please let me know whether it is gone.

    06/01/2026¶

    CHANGES¶

    • The slave tab is now properly wired. It was built, but not actually connected to the button!

    05/31/2026¶

    CHANGES¶

    • The Archonexus relocation endgame is now fully keyboard-accessible: choosing your colonists and items, picking your new colony's world tile, and reforming your ideoligion all work with the mod.
    • During that endgame you can create, load, or fully edit your new colony's ideoligion, just like the builder you use when starting a world.
    • Arrow-key navigation now names the roof above you instead of a generic "roofed" or "underground", so a thin rock roof, a solid overhead mountain, and a player-built roof are easy to tell apart.
    • Fish population reports moved from the terrain key (2) to the resource key (3), alongside plants and minerals. Key 3 now lists only the resources relevant to your tile.
    • You can now banish a colonist, and execute a guilty one. Both live on the Social section of a pawn's inspection screen and on the Actions tab of their info card, each with the game's own warning about what will happen.
    • The scanner now points you at the nearest tile of a patch (a biome, a vein of ore, a stretch of soil, a road or river) instead of its center, so you hear how far its closest edge is and whether you are already standing on it. Press Home to jump to that nearest tile; once you are on the patch, press Home again to jump to its center. The colony map and the world map work the same way.
    • You can now load colonists and items into a map portal the same way you load a caravan or transport pod. This covers entering an ancient complex, going down an Anomaly pit gate or insect lair, and hauling your loot back out through the exit. The enter and exit commands now open a Pawns and Items screen with full keyboard navigation. Before, that screen opened silently and you could only move pawns in one at a time through the right-bracket menu, with no way to bring items along.

    BUG FIXES¶

    • In auto-jump mode, the scanner no longer reports every place it takes you as being right on top of you. It used to measure the distance after it had already moved you onto the target, so each jump sounded like it was zero tiles away. It now simply announces what is on each tile as you flip through the scanner.
    • Cleaned up the building inspection menu: the "Power Control", "Breakdown Status", and "Power" items are gone. They either duplicated the Overview or let you do things you shouldn't (flip power instantly). Use the toggle power command (G) and a colonist will flick the switch as normal.
    • Number keys, arrow keys, and letter shortcuts no longer "leak" through to whatever is underneath an open menu. With a menu open, pressing a number could announce tile info, and letter shortcuts (like Alt+A or the ] orders key) could fire or type into a search box. This is now theoretically fixed across every menu.
    • Typeahead search now works the same in every menu. While you are searching, Up/Down step through the matches and Home/End jump to the first and last, all without clearing your search, and each tells you where you are (for example "match 2 of 3").
    • Typing no longer jumps to the wrong list when one menu sits on top of another. Float menus opened over a menu (like the bills "add recipe" picker), menus opened over a pawn's inspection screen (Operations, surgery pickers, Health Settings), and the quantity boxes in caravan and pod-loading dialogs now all search the menu you are actually looking at.
    • The pawn and building inspection screens now support typeahead. Start typing to jump through the tree, including weapons, apparel, and inventory items by name. Inspection search also matches what's inside a node, not just its name.
    • In the drug policy editor, Escape now clears an active search before closing, and the drug list reports how many matches your search found. The growing-zone plant picker also steps through search results correctly.
    • The saved-ideoligion load list no longer locks up when it's empty; Escape closes it normally.
    • When editing an ideoligion's base precepts (cannibalism, corpses, drug use, and the like), you can now press Delete to remove a precept you set, following the game's own rules about which can be dropped.
    • Fixed Alt-key shortcuts like Alt+R not working on some non-US keyboard layouts, including AZERTY.

    05/25/2026¶

    This is a milestone beta release for me. As of this update, every RimWorld DLC is essentially fully accessible with the mod. Anything still rough is either a convenience (mostly around gravship building) or a bug, and bugs get fixed as they come in. The headline this time is the custom ideoligion builder, but it was only possible because of a big overhaul to how text boxes work, so let's start there.

    CHANGES¶

    • Text boxes are now real text boxes, or as close as we can get. There is an actual cursor you can move through your text with the arrow keys, or jump word by word by holding Ctrl. Hold Shift while moving to select text, and Backspace removes the character before the cursor (or whatever you have selected). The mod now also knows the difference between a single-line box, like a name, and a multi-line box, like a description, so in a multi-line box you can press Shift+Enter to start a new line. When you finish editing with Enter, the mod reads back what the field now says. Typeahead should also work on non-English keyboard layouts now, though I have not been able to test that myself.
    • The big one: you can now build your own custom ideoligions, just like sighted players do on the ideoligion setup screen. This has been a long time coming. The builder has two tabs, and you switch between them with Tab. The first tab lists every ideoligion in the game; the second tab shows whichever one is selected. Select your own ideoligion and the second tab becomes a full editor; select someone else's and it is a read-only viewer. The editor is one big expandable list. The game crams an enormous amount of information onto this screen, so when you expand an item you can arrow through it line by line and hear all of it at your own pace. Most items can be changed by pressing Enter on them, including your ideoligion's name and descriptions, so when in doubt, press Enter and see what happens. To move on to the next setup screen, press Alt+S. The right bracket key ( ] ) opens a menu of extra options, including saving an ideoligion you have built so you can load it again in future games.
    • Fair warning: building an ideoligion is genuinely complicated, even for sighted players. There are a LOT of moving parts. If you are new to the game, you may want to get comfortable first. But I'm not your mom. Do whatever you want!
    • Fluid ideoligions work the same way in-game. Once yours has earned enough development points to reform, open the ideoligion viewer and press Enter on the item that shows your development points to begin.
    • The focused Work view now reads the selected colonist's name when it opens.
    • The colonist skills table (Alt+P) now tells you how much experience each skill has toward its next level, not just the level. On a paused game you can rest on a skill and hear the numbers change as colonists learn.
    • The starting characters screen now gives each colonist a Relations category, listing who they're tied to in your starting group and how
    • "Daughter," "Lover," and the like - with each pawn's opinion in the tooltip. Press Enter on a relation to jump straight to that pawn.
    • That same screen now has a Team Skills tab. Press Tab to switch to it for a quick rundown of which starting pawn is best at each skill.
    • The world map scanner now finds rivers, not just roads.
    • Walls (built walls, natural rock, and closed doors) now play their own navigation sound instead of the sound of the ground beneath them. Terrain sounds were also evened out so the volumes are more consistent.

    BUG FIXES¶

    • Returning to the main menu from inside a game while on the world map no longer leaves the menu unresponsive. The Enter key works again right away, so you can pick a menu option without getting stuck.

    05/24/2026¶

    BUG FIXES¶

    • Screen reader speech works again on setups where JAWS (or another reader) had gone silent. A recent version of the underlying Prism speech library began treating NVDA as available even when it wasn't running, so on machines without NVDA active it selected NVDA and spoke nothing. The mod is now pinned to the last known-good Prism version until the upstream fix ships.

    INFO CARDS EVERYWHERE (Alt+I)¶

    The biggest addition in this patch for Vanilla Rimworld: you can now press Alt+I in nearly any menu or screen to open the info card for whatever you have selected. Info cards are the detailed stat sheets that sighted players access by clicking the (i) button -- and until now, screen reader users had few ways to reach them. They're now available in 15 different contexts:

    • Map cursor (when hovering over buildings, items, terrain, or pawns)
    • Architect menu (get stats on a building before you place it)
    • Plant selection menu
    • Storage settings (check details on any item in the filter list)
    • Bills menu and bill configuration
    • Gizmo bar (abilities, build commands, and more)

    • Research menu and research detail view (inspect unlocked items and recipes)
    • Colony animals menu
    • Wildlife menu
    • Float menus (right-click context menus)
    • Pawn inspection: gear, health conditions, and genes all support Alt+I
    • Inside info cards themselves -- follow hyperlinks to drill into related entries

I want to extend this across the entire mod, but right now, things that are very clearly items / buildings / plants don't say this since it should be assumed that you can press alt i.

    • When a stat entry links to multiple items, a selection menu appears so you can choose which one to inspect
