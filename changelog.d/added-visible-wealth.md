[Visible Wealth](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3461137081) is now supported. The wealth breakdown reads as a real tree you can open and close by category, the flat list is a table of every item with its value and percentage, and the pie chart reads as a list of its slices in order with their values and share of your total wealth. Every toolbar option, the search box and the play-settings button work from the keyboard, and the window opens and closes categories right along with you. Its Shift+4 hotkey no longer opens the window while you are typing or using a menu.
