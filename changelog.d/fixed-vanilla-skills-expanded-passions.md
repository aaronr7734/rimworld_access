With [Vanilla Skills Expanded](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3400246558), every passion is now spoken by name everywhere skills are read, including the mod's extra passions like apathy that used to go unannounced or come out as a number in growth moments. Each skill in the Skills tab also says how fast its passion makes the pawn learn and forget, with the passion's description one level down.
