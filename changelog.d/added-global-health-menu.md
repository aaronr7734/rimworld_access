Added a health overview, opened with Alt+Shift+H on the map or from the F12 menu. It lists every downed, bleeding, or injured pawn on the map under Downed and bleeding, Downed, Bleeding, and Injured, most urgent first, with a short summary on each heading. Tab reaches checkboxes for which groups to show, and the list keeps itself up to date while open. Press Enter on a pawn to jump to them and open their health tab, or Alt+I for their info card.
