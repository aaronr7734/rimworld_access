Added a health overview, opened with Alt+Shift+H on the map or from the F12 menu. It lists every downed, bleeding, injured, or sick pawn on the map under Downed and bleeding, Downed, Bleeding, Injured, and Sick, your own pawns first and then the most urgent, with a short summary on each heading that also says how many need rescue or tending. Under Sick, each pawn lists their diseases with severity, immunity, and tending, and Sick moves to the top while a disease is life-threatening. Tab reaches checkboxes for which groups to show, and the list keeps itself up to date while open. Press Enter on a pawn to jump to them and open their health tab, or on a disease to land right on it, and Alt+I for an info card.
