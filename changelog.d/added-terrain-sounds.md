The map cursor has new terrain sounds for sand, rough stone, smooth stone, ice, lava, marsh and metal floors. Sterile, steel, silver and gold tiles, ship substructure and heavy bridges now sound like metal, rough and smoothed rock sound different, and lava no longer sounds like water. Flood water, orbital and mechanoid platforms and a few other surfaces that used to play nothing now have a sound, and terrain added by other mods picks its sound from what the game knows about it.
