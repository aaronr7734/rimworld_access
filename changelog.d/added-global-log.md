Added the global log, opened with Alt+L or from the F12 menu. It lists every social and combat event in the world, not just one pawn's, and remembers far more than the game's own log. Filter by social or combat, by whether your colonists, your animals, or anyone else is involved, and pick newest or oldest first. Type to search, arrow through the matches in order, and press Enter to read around one. Each entry can jump you to the pawns involved.
