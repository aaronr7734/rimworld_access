Added a gear menu on Alt+Shift+G that lists every weapon and piece of apparel worn or held by anyone on the map, one row per item with who has it, like "Duster (normal), worn by Bob, Dylan, Harry". Press Right arrow to step into those pawns, and Enter on one to jump to them with their gear tab open on that item. Tab reaches checkboxes for which groups to show, and the menu keeps itself up to date while open.
