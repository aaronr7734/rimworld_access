[Numbers](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1414302321) is now supported. Needs, skills, psyfocus, neural heat, bandwidth, ideoligion, disease, abilities, treatment, equipment, inventory, prisoner interaction and operations columns all read their values, and Enter opens the same menus a click would, so you can drop an item, pick a prisoner interaction or add a surgery bill right from the table. On the header row, Delete removes a column and Ctrl+Left and Ctrl+Right move it, and Numbers remembers the layout just like it does for mouse edits.
